Scientists Discover New Deep-Sea Species Near Pacific Trench

Published 2 hours ago — World Section

Marine biologists aboard the research vessel Nautilus have discovered three previously unknown species living near the Mariana Trench at depths exceeding 8,000 meters.

The team, led by Dr. Elena Vasquez of the Oceanographic Institute, used remotely operated vehicles to capture footage of the organisms during a six-week expedition.

"We were astonished by the biodiversity at these depths," Dr. Vasquez said in a press conference. "These species have adapted to survive under extreme pressure and complete darkness."

The findings, published in the journal Nature Ecology & Evolution, include a translucent jellyfish-like creature, a bioluminescent fish, and a previously unknown type of bacterial colony.
